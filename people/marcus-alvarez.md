# Marcus Alvarez

**Role:** Principal Software Architect

Marcus Alvarez serves as Northstar Engineering's Principal Software Architect and chairs the Architecture Review Board. He is responsible for guiding long-term technical direction across the Atlas platform and helping engineering teams make design decisions that balance today's needs with tomorrow's challenges.

Marcus is known for asking difficult questions rather than providing immediate answers. He encourages engineers to explore alternatives, justify their reasoning, and carefully consider the long-term consequences of architectural decisions.

## Responsibilities

- Defines system-wide architectural direction
- Reviews significant design proposals
- Maintains Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)
- Guides platform modernization efforts
- Mentors engineering teams on software design

## Design Philosophy

Marcus believes that good architecture is rarely about finding a perfect solution. Instead, it is about understanding tradeoffs, documenting assumptions, and making decisions that can evolve as the system changes.

He frequently reminds engineers:

> "Every design decision solves one problem while creating another. Your job is to choose the right tradeoff."

## Frequently Asks

- What assumptions are we making?
- Which responsibilities belong together?
- How will this design change over time?
- What are we optimizing for?
- What happens if this requirement changes next year?
- Could this responsibility live somewhere else?
- Are we increasing or reducing coupling?
- How would a new engineer understand this design?

## Current Focus

Marcus is leading the Atlas 4.0 modernization initiative. Current priorities include improving service modularity, reducing technical debt, standardizing architectural practices, and helping teams evolve the platform without disrupting existing customers.

## Notes

Marcus values thoughtful discussion and evidence over quick answers. He rarely rejects an idea outright, but he often asks engineers to reconsider their assumptions or explore additional alternatives before making a final recommendation.
