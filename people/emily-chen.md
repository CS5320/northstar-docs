# Emily Chen

**Role:** Director of Engineering

Emily leads the engineering organization at Northstar Solutions and sponsors the Atlas modernization initiative.

She values thoughtful software design, collaboration, and evidence-based decision making. While she expects engineers to justify their recommendations, she encourages experimentation and believes there is rarely a single correct design solution.

## Responsibilities

- Engineering leadership
- Technical strategy
- Architecture review
- Cross-team coordination

## Frequently Asks

- What problem are we trying to solve?
- What evidence supports this recommendation?
- How will this affect future development?
